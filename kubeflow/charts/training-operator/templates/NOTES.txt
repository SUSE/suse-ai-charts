** DRA (Dynamic Resource Allocation) prerequisites **
The CRD schema now includes resourceClaims and resources.claims fields,
which require the DynamicResourceAllocation feature gate to be enabled.

- Kubernetes >= 1.31 required (feature stable in 1.32).
- A DRA driver (e.g. gpu.nvidia.com DeviceClass) must be installed.
- Create a ResourceClaimTemplate in each job namespace before submitting
  jobs that reference spec.pytorchReplicaSpecs.*.template.spec.resourceClaims.

Jobs that do not use DRA are unaffected.
